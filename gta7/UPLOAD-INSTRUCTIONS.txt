HARBOR HEAT — YOUR TWO HOSTS

FRONTEND: upload the CONTENTS of harbor-heat-upload/ to lowk.cooking/gta7/.
BACKEND: upload the CONTENTS of harbor-heat-backend/ to
         sandeepshenoy.dev public_html/harbor-heat-api/.

Check: https://sandeepshenoy.dev/harbor-heat-api/setup.php
Play:  https://lowk.cooking/gta7/

The client URL and CORS allowlist are already configured for these domains.
Keep hidden .htaccess files. PHP 8.1+ and pdo_sqlite are required only on
sandeepshenoy.dev. No game-source edits, passwords, Node.js, Composer or
manual database setup are needed. The backend creates its private storage.

The frontend folder also contains PHP for a same-host deployment; lowk.cooking
only needs index.html and assets/. The extra PHP copies can be omitted there.
The actual multiplayer backend on lowk.cooking is never used: the client selects
the sandeepshenoy.dev URL automatically for that hostname.
