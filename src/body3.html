(13) The method

          
01/ 06

          

            
Preheat the GPU
Ask the browser for a WebGL context. Compile thirty-one shaders while the loader counts to a hundred.

            
Whisk the noise
Hash a grid, interpolate, stack five octaves. Every texture on this page starts as the same four lines.

            
Fold the domain
Feed the noise back into its own coordinates. Twice. That's where the marble, smoke and fire come from.

            
Sear at sixty
Sixteen milliseconds a frame. Resolution scales itself down before a single frame drops.

            
Season with sound
Twenty-seven field recordings, crossfaded into seamless loops and mixed by where you are on the page.

            
Plate. Serve.
Concatenate everything into one HTML file. No bundler, no framework, no network after the fonts.
